Game of Life

A board without edges that never slows down.

An Android simulator: you draw living cells with your finger, and they are born and die by four simple rules. I built all of it myself — the engine, gameplay, interface and the store release.

  • Zoom out to hundreds of thousands of cells — still smooth, even on an older phone.
  • An experimental turbo mode leaps thousands of generations per frame.
  • A pattern library, save slots and a tutorial that teaches the rules through drawing.

Grave

You only see what’s lit — and the monsters hear every step.

A finished 2D top-down survival horror, built by a team of four as our engineering project. I wrote three of its systems: the vision cone, the dungeon generator and A* pathfinding.

  • Vision cone — outside the light, monsters disappear from view.
  • Procedural dungeons — the same seed rebuilds the identical dungeon.
  • A* pathfinding — monsters chase you without stalling the game.
